Warden Kesh

Pick up to 4 towers. In battle, buying a tower gives you a random one from this loadout.

How to play
  • Merge: drag a tower onto a matching one (same type and level) to level it up, to a maximum of 8.
  • Branch: at level 4, choose one of two specializations. Towers with different branches can't merge.
  • Runes: buy runes to your bench and drag them onto towers. Each tower has 1 socket, 2 at level 3 and 3 at level 5. Merge two matching runes on the bench to tier them up.
  • Rune fusions: some rune pairs on one tower unlock a bonus. Examples: Ember + Rime = Thermal Shock, and Volt + Haste = Overdrive.
  • Stacking: 3 or more towers of one type give that type +20% damage, and 5 or more give +40%.
  • Send: in a duel, sending attackers raises your income every 6 seconds and pressures your opponent.
  • Counters: armor shrugs off small hits, Bulwark shields take double damage from splash and burns, and Wraiths are hidden until a Railgun or Focus rune spots them.
