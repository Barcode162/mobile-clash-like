How to play
  • Buy drops a random level 1 tower on a free tile.
  • Drag two matching towers together (same type, same level) to merge them into the next level, up to level 8.
  • Stack synergy: 3 or more towers of one type give that type +20% damage, and 5 or more give +40%. Merging lowers the count, so choose carefully.
  • Mixed army: 4 different types on the field give +10% attack speed, and all 5 give +20%.
  • Drag a tower onto Sell to get coins back. Drag it onto an empty tile to move it, or onto another tower to swap.
  • Every 5th wave brings tanks and every 10th wave brings a boss.
  • Clearing waves earns Crowns. Spend them here on Town Hall, tower cards and buildings.
  • Tower kills earn mastery stars, worth +4% damage each, up to 5.
